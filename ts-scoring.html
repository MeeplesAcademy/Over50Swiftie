Taylor Swift Song & Album Scoring
Score every song · rank every album · build your dream playlists
Score each song 0–5. Album averages update live, checkboxes below feed your Eras Album and Perfect Album lists at the bottom of the page.

1. Score songs. Click an album tile below to open its song list in the panel underneath the grid. Give each song a score from 0–5. The song and its score change color as you go — that's your running conditional formatting.

2. Watch albums rank themselves. Each tile shows a live average of that album's scored songs. The "My Favorite Albums" section below sorts all 12 highest to lowest, automatically.

3. Build your Eras Album. Check the box next to one song per album (plus one bonus pick from anywhere) to assemble a 13-track "Eras" playlist in the results section.

4. Build your Perfect Album. Every song you score a 5 shows up in the Perfect Album pool. Check 13 of them to fill your Perfect Album's 13 track slots.

5. Save your progress. Your scores auto-save in this browser as you go. Use Save scores to file to download a backup you can re-upload later (even on a different device or browser), and Save PDF snapshot to keep a dated printable copy of your results.

I recently changed it to be an interactive webpage so I could have more fun with it and Swifties like you could get more use out of it. (It’s most recently been updated to include the TLOAS: The Encore songs.)

She was incredibly sweet and I’ve followed her career as a fan ever since.
